    About Supreme Borehole Drilling Services

    A borehole and water solutions company based in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, serving homes, businesses, farms and communities.

    Who we are

    Supreme Borehole Drilling Services is based at 217B Abak Road, Uyo. We help customers get dependable water through borehole drilling, installation, pump installation, system maintenance and rehabilitation of existing boreholes.

    We work with homeowners, businesses, property developers, construction companies, farms and institutions. Every site is different, so we start by listening to what you need and what the property allows.
